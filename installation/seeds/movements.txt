# Etymon — the installation's score.
#
# Ten movements. The piece moves through them in order, then loops. Each line
# is a seed word whose ancestry is dived for and raised out of the ocean.
#
# Format:  <movement title> :: <subtitle shown beneath>
#          eng word
#          tur word
# A bare word defaults to English.
#
# The selection is the argument of the piece. Every word we have invented for
# this technological moment is a salvaged older word, and the installation's
# job is to make that visible: you watch "hallucinate" sink through Latin
# alucinari, "to wander in the mind", and surface again as a machine's failure
# mode.
